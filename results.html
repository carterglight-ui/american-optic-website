Common questions

How does the pricing work?

The numbers shown are starting points for a typical project of that scope. Your final quote depends on shoot days, locations, talent, travel and how many final cuts you need. We give you a fixed, itemised quote before anything is booked.

How long does a project take?

A Starter project is usually 1–2 weeks from kickoff to delivery. Basic runs 2–4 weeks. Top-Tier productions with casting and multiple shoot days typically run 4–8 weeks. Rush timelines are possible — just ask.

Do you travel for shoots?

Yes. We're based in Florida and shoot across the Southeast regularly, and nationally for larger productions. Travel is added to the quote at cost.

Who owns the finished video and footage?

You own the final delivered videos outright, with full usage rights for advertising and organic use. Raw footage archival can be added to any tier.
